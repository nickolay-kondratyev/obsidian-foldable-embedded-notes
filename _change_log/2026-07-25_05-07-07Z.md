The whole-line marker regex anchored the `-` to end-of-line, so a stray trailing
space silently disabled fold-by-default in Live Preview while reading mode
accepted it. Invisible difference, invisible failure.

- `src/livePreview/markedEmbedLines.ts`: regex is now `/^!\[\[[^\]\n]+\]\]-[ \t]*$/`,
  and `dashFrom` derives from the dash's real index (`lastIndexOf("-")`, unambiguous
  because only whitespace may follow) rather than "last character of the line".
- The `Decoration.replace` still covers exactly the dash and nothing more: reading
  mode's `stripFoldMarker` leaves trailing whitespace in the text, so Live Preview
  does too. Deliberate, and pinned by an exact-text assertion.
- e2e: new fixture line plus exact-text assertions in
  `e2e/live-preview-foldable-embeds.e2e.ts` — the previous `trimEnd().endsWith("-")`
  helper could not distinguish "trailing space survived" from "dash and space both
  swallowed". Verified red before green. The line lookup now throws on an ambiguous
  match instead of silently taking the first.
