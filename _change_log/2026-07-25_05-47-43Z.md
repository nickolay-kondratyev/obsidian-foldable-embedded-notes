The post-processor renders every embed BODY, including bodies inside Live Preview
widgets — DOM that is Obsidian's and IS reused. With no removal path, disabling the
plugin left `fen-embed`, `fen-folded` and a chevron on nested embeds plus a LIVE click
listener from the dead instance: it kept folding, kept the unloaded plugin and its
`FoldStateStore` retained, and its leaked `preventDefault()` stopped the click from
opening the embedded note. The leftover CSS class then made both wiring guards bail, so
a re-enabled instance could never rewire.

- `src/foldableEmbedMark.ts` — one embed's marks plus its `AbortController`, with the
  exact inverse in `onunload`. Owned BOTH by a `MarkdownRenderChild` (per-render cleanup)
  and by a processor-level registry unloaded from `Plugin.onunload()`; the render child
  alone was MEASURED not to fire on plugin disable, because disabling leaves the DOM in
  place and the documented unload trigger is `containerEl` removal.
- `src/wiredElements.ts` — `WeakSet` wiring guard shared by both modes, replacing the
  CSS-class guards that a dead instance's leftovers could poison.
- e2e: nested-embed fixture in the Live Preview teardown test, written first and
  confirmed red for the right reason. Full suite 43/43.

DEVIATION from the ticket's design, pending human sign-off: part 3 (Live Preview
`destroy()` sweeping nested embeds) was NOT done — it is redundant once the
post-processor owns its own teardown, and would orphan a live post-processor listener on
view recreation. Reviewed and accepted by IMPLEMENTATION_REVIEWER.

KNOWN LIMITATION, measured: a re-enabled plugin only rewires nested embeds on the next
real render, so an already-open note keeps unfoldable nested embeds until reopened.
Follow-up ticket filed (load-time adoption), along with four smaller ones the review
surfaced.
