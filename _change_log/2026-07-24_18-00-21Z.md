Throwaway prototyping (8 e2e tests, all green, then deleted) proved: reading-view embeds are postprocessor-reachable spans; the ![[x]]- marker renders as a strippable trailing text node; CSS-class fold collapses 188px->24px; title-bar click toggle and fold-all/expand-all commands work. Product decisions: native title bar + chevron UI, session-memory fold state, commands-only (no extra UI), strict marker parsing.
