Adds Live Preview (editing mode) support to the foldable-embeds plugin, previously reading-mode only.

- `src/livePreview/` — CM6 editor extension in 3 modules: whole-line marker scan + dash-hiding decoration (gated on `editorLivePreviewField`), fold state as a position-mapping `RangeSet`, and a ViewPlugin projecting state onto Obsidian embed widgets via a `contentDOM` MutationObserver (embeds render async, outside CM update cycles).
- `src/embedFoldDom.ts` — extracted the shared `styles.css` DOM contract (class names, chevron, title-click, teardown `unmark`) used by BOTH modes. Readiness logic, fold-state identity and marker parsing stay per-mode; they genuinely differ.
- Live Preview owns only TOP-LEVEL embeds; nested ones stay with the reading-mode post-processor (`posAtDOM` is only line-accurate, so a nested embed would otherwise fold its parent).
- Deliberate limits, documented in README: the `-` marker applies only when the embed is the entire line, and fold state is per-mode/per-session.
- 12 new e2e tests (23/23 total, real Obsidian 1.12.7); lint and build clean.
