Prototype (throwaway, archived under `.ai_out/live-preview-foldable-embeds/master/prototype/`) proved in real Obsidian 1.12.7: existing `styles.css` collapses Live Preview embeds unchanged, the `-` marker can be hidden with a CM6 `Decoration.replace`, fold state survives edits via a `StateField`, and click-to-fold works.

Constraints found: Obsidian renders embeds asynchronously (a `contentDOM` MutationObserver is required), `posAtDOM` is only line-accurate, Obsidian swallows title clicks before CM sees them, and a raw-text marker scan false-positives inside code spans.

Human decision: GO with WHOLE-LINE `![[x]]-` marker semantics in Live Preview and per-mode fold state. No shipped source changed (only `eslint.config.mts` now ignores `.ai_out`).
