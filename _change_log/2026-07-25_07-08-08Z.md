Ticket nid_7qbtubxk89team9oadnl3hanr_e.

WAS: `sourcePath::L<line>::<src>::#<indexWithinSection>`. The line is a POSITION and nothing mapped
it across edits, so inserting lines above a folded embed LOST the fold, and deleting lines above two
same-`src` embeds handed one embed the other`s fold (both MEASURED on Obsidian 1.12.7).

NOW: `sourcePath::occ::<link>::#<ordinal>` (`src/embedFoldKeys.ts`), derived from
`app.metadataCache` through a narrow injected `ReadEmbeds` port — the whole `App` is deliberately NOT
passed in. The cache entry is located by POSITION, so the unmeasured `span.src` vs `EmbedCache.link`
join is off the correctness path.

Cold-cache handoff: the vault index is COLD for the first render after Obsidian launches, so the
occurrence key is not always derivable. Rather than wait or retry, a key also reports the positional
key it supersedes, and `FoldStateStore.adoptRecordingOf` lets the first render that CAN derive an
occurrence key reclaim a fold recorded during the boot window. Without this the change would have
REGRESSED the boot window relative to the old cache-independent key.

Still open by design: `nid_z4jq8me8mhstojozeua8fufdr_e` (deleting an earlier same-`src` embed shifts
the ordinal, so the fold is still inherited) and `nid_zqaxj18jbxwnazzz8aeggz91u_e` (nested embeds
share one key). The key shape composes with both: opaque string, stable delimiter, parseable
sourcePath prefix.

Verified: failing-first e2e (`e2e/reading-mode-fold-key.e2e.ts`), and the blocking-fix revert proved
red 4-of-8 runs without the handoff. Lint and build clean; full e2e 49 passed across repeated runs.
