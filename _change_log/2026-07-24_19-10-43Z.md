Obsidian's `right-triangle` glyph (path `M3 8L12 17L21 8`) points DOWN at rest, not right. The prior CSS assumed a right-pointing base, so it rendered expanded=left, collapsed=down. Reworked `styles.css` to mirror core's `.collapse-icon` convention: no rotation when expanded (▼), `rotate(-90deg)` when `is-collapsed` (▶). Verified by extracting the exact glyph + core rotation from obsidian.asar 1.12.7 and screenshotting both states.
